## What performs (2025–2026 benchmarks)

**Instagram** (engagement against followers):
- Carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, single images 0.37% ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram), 2025, about 67.5 million posts). Q3 2026: 0.50%, 0.48%, 0.35%.
- Reels reach 29% more accounts than carousels and 212% more than single images. Single-image reach fell 22% in a year. Carousels earn 9× the saves of single images ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-research-study/), 2026, 24.4 million posts).
- Stories: 23.8% of viewers leave on the first frame. Video Stories reach more than images ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks), 2025).
- Hashtags are capped at 5 per post since December 2025, and Instagram says they don't add reach ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)).

**LinkedIn company pages** (engagement against impressions):
- Documents 7.0%, multi-image 6.45%, video 6.0%, single image 5.3%, text 4.5%, polls 4.2%, links 3.25% ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/linkedin), 2025). Q3 2026: documents and multi-image 6.4%, single image 4.7%.
- Pages with 1,000–5,000 followers average 525 impressions per document, 490 per multi-image post and 315 per single image.
- Hashtags add no reach. Links in the text are suppressed, so they go in the first comment.

**Both:**
- Captions of 90 words or more do worst. Image posts barely react to caption length ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-caption-length-best-practices/)).
- Times, local ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-social-media), 2026): Instagram is best on weekday mornings (Thursday 9 AM), with a lift around 6 PM. LinkedIn is best on Wednesday at 4 PM and Friday at 3–4 PM. No credible study covers Indian festival days.
- Festival greetings: no performance data exists. Commentary criticises the same templated greeting reposted with a different logo ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/analysis/indian-corporates-and-their-grand-ritual-of-festival-branding)). The Diwali work that gets praised carries one specific idea or human story ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/feature/vigyapanti/fireworks-lights-creative-spark-brand-campaigns-play-safe-diwali/2766415), October 2025).
- Clutter lowers attention to the brand and liking, and a crafted layout raises both ([Pieters, Wedel & Batra](https://repository.tilburguniversity.edu/items/3fddf97a-469e-4d38-9c26-8dbd00c750b2), Journal of Marketing, 2010).

## What competitors post (scan of 8 November 2026)

Peers checked: Zoho, Freshworks, Kissflow, Chargebee and Ideas2IT (Chennai); Codewave and GoodWorkLabs (Bengaluru); Simform (Ahmedabad); Lollypop Design and Yellow Slice (design studios). LinkedIn blocks logged-out reading of company pages and Instagram isn't indexed, so **no post-level engagement numbers could be collected**. Only follower counts were confirmed: Chargebee has 346,986 LinkedIn followers and Ideas2IT 75,665. Everything below comes from what is publicly visible.

- **Brand pages go quiet or generic at Diwali.** No peer's official Diwali 2025 post surfaced. The festive content lives on employees' feeds instead: office celebrations, ethnic wear, gift unboxings. Where small firms do post, they reach for stock phrases: "Happy Diwali from all of us at…", "joy, success and prosperity".
- **Festivals are regional.** Chennai firms and their staff say "Deepavali", and Kissflow puts its festive effort into Pongal (one of its posts says Pongal is bigger than Diwali for Tamils). Simform and Codewave went big on Navratri instead.
- **The festive posts that stood out tied the festival to something real:**
  - Freshworks' "This Diwali, Freshworks is lighting up airports": airport ads built on a confirmed Gartner placement.
  - Lollypop's Deepavali envelope in 14 Indian languages, which doubled as portfolio work.
- **Everyday mix:** product and AI news, analyst recognition, events and conferences, culture posts, regional festival greetings. Design studios post tips as carousels.
- **Where the reach is:** trade press says most 2025 Diwali brand activity ran as Instagram Reels and video. In one industry survey, 43% of consumers distrust ads that look AI-made.

**What we take from it:**
- Show craft instead of templates. That is where peers are weakest, and it doubles as a Creatives portfolio piece.
- Say "Deepavali" alongside "Diwali", and plan for Pongal (14 January 2027).
- Use no stock phrases.
- Make a Reel for reach.
- Keep images looking photographed rather than synthetic.
- Ask Rohit to reshare company posts from his own LinkedIn profile, since festive engagement sits on people's feeds rather than brand pages.
- Never borrow a proof point we can't confirm.

To get real numbers, spend 10 minutes logged in. Open the LinkedIn pages of Kissflow, Chargebee, Ideas2IT and Lollypop and note the reactions on their posts around 20 October 2025 (Diwali 2025). Then add them here.

## Rules for hitroo posts

1. **Format:** on Instagram, a carousel of 3–5 slides at 1080 × 1350, plus a Story; a Reel whenever we have motion. On LinkedIn, the same slides as a PDF document (or multi-image). A single image only when the idea needs exactly one frame.
2. **One idea per post, in the site's language:** paper, clear glass, one ember accent, a two-beat headline with the second beat in stone, the wordmark at the foot ([designs.md](../designs.md)). No templates, gold, glitter or stock festival art.
3. **Captions:** under 40 words, with the point in the first 125 characters (what shows before "more"). Always "hitroo" in lowercase, calm, with no exclamation marks, and no stock phrases ("from all of us at", "joy, success and prosperity"). On Instagram 2–3 hashtags (never more than 5); on LinkedIn 0–2 and no links in the text. Don't name the city: no "Chennai" in public copy, captions or bios (owner, 8 November). Hashtags such as #Deepavali are fine.
4. **Alt text** on every image.
5. **Timing:** Instagram at 9–11 AM or around 6 PM IST; LinkedIn on weekday afternoons. Then trust our own numbers over these defaults.
6. **Reels must feel like Reels.** Follow the rg viral-shorts recipe (`~/Desktop/rg/viral-shorts/RECIPE.md`):
   - motion and the hook on frame 0, and something new every 1–3 s;
   - type that arrives word by word;
   - cuts on the music's beats;
   - music plus a sound on every move, mixed to −14 LUFS.

   Build them in HyperFrames, one project per post (`<post>/reel/`). The owner turned down a slow-zoom slideshow (8 November).
7. **Measure:** a week after each post, record reach, likes, comments, saves and shares here (`node media/tools/zernio.mjs recent <accountId>`).
8. **The owner approves every post** before it is published.
